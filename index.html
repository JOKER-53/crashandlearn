Quickstart & Reproduction

Reproduce training, evaluate checkpoints, and generate your own telemetry HUD recordings with pure Python CLI tools.

# Benchmark the pre-trained Default model across 10 episodes $ venv/bin/python evaluate.py --model models/ppo_default --episodes 10 # Inspect the reward hacking hover policy $ venv/bin/python evaluate.py --model models/ppo_hack --episodes 10 # Verify the fixed reward shaping model $ venv/bin/python evaluate.py --model models/ppo_fixed --episodes 10
