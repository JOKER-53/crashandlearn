Specification Gaming & Reward Shaping in Continuous Control: An Empirical Analysis

Kadhir • Open Source Research Repository

Abstract: Reinforcement learning agents optimize exclusively for the scalar reward signal provided by the environment designer, frequently uncovering degenerate local optima known as specification gaming or reward hacking. In this work, we train Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) agents on the standard LunarLander-v3 benchmark to study reward hacking in practice. We demonstrate that introducing an intuitive survival incentive (+1.5/step) induces an exploitation loophole where the agent learns to hover indefinitely rather than risk touchdown. We subsequently derive and verify a cost-penalized potential-based shaping function that eliminates the exploit and restores a 100% safe touchdown rate.

Analytical Formulation

Reward Shaping Functions

Formal mathematical definitions comparing the environment's canonical shaping against the intentional survival vulnerability and the penalized fix.

Canonical Shaping (Default) Gymnasium v3
R_t = \Delta\Phi(s_t) - c_{\text{main}} - c_{\text{side}} + 10(l_1 + l_2)

Where $\Phi(s) = -100\sqrt{x^2+y^2} - 100\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2} - 100|\theta|$. Progress towards the landing pad center generates positive reward differences ($\Delta\Phi > 0$), while engine firings incur fuel cost deductions.

Survival Hack Formulation Exploitation
R_{\text{hack}} = R_t + \beta_{\text{survival}} \quad (\beta = +1.5/\text{frame})

Because an episode truncates at $T = 1,000$ steps, hovering yields $1,000 \times 1.5 = +1,500$ points. Landing terminates the episode prematurely, granting only a one-time $+100$ bonus. The rational policy avoids touchdown.

Penalized Shaping (Fixed) Alignment Fix
R_{\text{fixed}} = R_t - c_{\text{step}} + \alpha_{\text{contact}}(l_1 + l_2)

Applying a per-step time tax ($c_{\text{step}} = -0.05$) directly penalizes lingering in mid-air. Ground contact sensors are rewarded ($\alpha = +0.2$), aligning policy gradient incentives with rapid, safe landing.

Reproduction Protocol

Command Line Execution

Every experiment is reproducible via modular CLI flags. No external cloud dependencies or GPUs required. 

# 1. Evaluate the official solved default model across 10 test seeds
venv/bin/python evaluate.py --model models/ppo_default --episodes 10

# 2. Benchmark the reward hacking policy (100% hover timeout rate)
venv/bin/python evaluate.py --model models/ppo_hack --episodes 10

# 3. Verify the fixed shaping recovery model (100% safe touchdown)
venv/bin/python evaluate.py --model models/ppo_fixed --episodes 10
