Precision Autonomous Rocket Landing via Residual Reinforcement Learning

Lead Researcher: Kadhiravan E. G. • Repository: github.com/JOKER-53/crashandlearn • License: MIT (Open Access)

We investigate policy alignment, specification gaming vulnerabilities, and deep neural guidance architectures in autonomous spacecraft descent. When an intuitive survival bonus is added, the policy discovers a specification loophole—hovering indefinitely for 1,000 frames to game accumulated rewards. We prove how potential-based reward shaping ($Ng\text{ et al.}$) restores alignment, achieving 100% precision touchdown with decoupled Actor-Critic deep residual networks.

Analytical Formulation

Potential-Based Reward Shaping & Loopholes

Formal mathematical definitions comparing the environment's canonical potential shaping against intentional specification gaming and the penalized recovery formulation.

Canonical Shaping (Default) Gymnasium v3
$$R_t = \Delta\Phi(s_t) - c_{\text{main}} - c_{\text{side}} + 10(l_1 + l_2)$$

Where potential $\Phi(s) = -100\sqrt{x^2+y^2} - 100\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2} - 100|\theta|$. Progress toward pad center yields positive reward deltas ($\Delta\Phi > 0$), while engine firings subtract fuel cost deductions.

Specification Gaming (Hack) Vulnerability
$$R_t = R_{\text{default}} + \beta_{\text{survival}} \quad (\beta = +1.50)$$

Adds a constant survival bonus. Over 1,000 steps, hovering generates $+1,500$ points—vastly overshadowing the $+100$ touchdown bonus. The optimal policy mathematically becomes: never land, hover until timeout.

Penalized Potential (Fixed) Aligned
$$R_t = R_{\text{default}} - c_{\text{step}} + 0.20(l_1 + l_2) \quad (c = 0.05)$$

Introduces an explicit time penalty $(-0.05/\text{frame})$ while augmenting leg sensor touchdown rewards. Hovering costs fuel and time; expeditious safe landing is strictly incentivized.
